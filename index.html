03

The home lab, and what runs on it.

Off the clock I build for my family: a dashboard on the wall, a house that handles its own routines, and Mecca, the server that runs it all. Pick a scenario, or tap any box.

The same thing in words: every screen in the house talks only to the dashboard server, which holds the passwords. Home Assistant controls the devices. Away from home, I connect through a private Tailscale network.

Software · 2026

Family Dashboard

A self-hosted, touch-first dashboard for every screen in our house. The family calendar comes first, then the weather from our own station, what's playing on the speakers, the thermostat, the state of the house with one-tap scenes, and the cameras.

  • A small server in Docker holds every credential and pushes live updates to all screens at once, so no screen ever stores a password.
  • Reachable only on the home network.
  • Built spec-first with AI-assisted engineering and covered by more than 400 automated tests.
  • Node.js
  • Docker
  • Home Assistant
  • CalDAV
  • AI-assisted engineering
